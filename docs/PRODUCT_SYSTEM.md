# HOY Lifestyle product system

## Product promise

**Was kann ich heute, jetzt und hier machen?**

HOY Lifestyle is a local decision layer, not a static tourism directory.

## Decision order

### 1. Hard gates
A candidate is removed or moved out of NOW when the entity is closed/suppressed/identity-conflicted; outside season/time; a confirmed age/height/licence/skill rule fails; verified weather/safety blocks it; booking lead time does not fit; or a definitive live claim is stale.

### 2. Organic score (100)
- intent/category: 25
- availability: 20
- time window: 15
- distance/travel: 10
- weather/context: 10
- explicit family/access preference: 5
- trust/freshness: 10
- diversity/serendipity: 5

Sponsorship is separate.

## Guest surfaces

## Operator surfaces
Claim/confirmation · Today status · regular hours/season · activity & price · booking/contact · family & eligibility · accessibility · specials/events · trust/data status · analytics later.

## Shared HOY platform patterns
Lifestyle should reuse platform-level ideas proven in Gastro: design-system/shell principles, trust/provenance/freshness, operator identity/roles, safe public data exposure, PWA boundaries, analytics/event contract and browser/release QA. Lifestyle-specific domain data remains separate.
